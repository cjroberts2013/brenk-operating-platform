Brenk Operating Platform — user guide

Tracking jobs from ServiceChannel through to getting paid

Getting around: sign in at app.brenkfacilityservices.com · the left menu switches between areas · the search box at the top filters Work Orders and Vendors (the Invoices tab has its own search).

The tabs at a glance

🏠
Dashboard
Morning overview — money tiles, the pipeline, and what's stuck.
📋
Work Orders
Every job, plus your own vendor and pricing tracking.
🧾
Invoices
Real SC invoices, plus jobs still waiting to be priced.
👷
Vendors
Your sub-vendor roster, their jobs, and a calendar.
⚙️
Settings
Your default markup % per trade.

How to do the everyday things

1

Handle a new job

  1. Open Work Orders and click the job ("Unassigned" is in red).
  2. Assign a sub-vendor.
  3. Text or call them, then Mark notified.
2

Price a completed job

  1. Open the finished job.
  2. In the Markup helper, enter cost + markup, or just type the total bill.
  3. Keep it under the NTE (turns red if not), then Save.
3

Invoice it and get paid

  1. Open in ServiceChannel and enter the invoice.
  2. The Invoices tab follows it Sent → Approved → Paid.
  3. Mark paid when the client pays.
4

See what's owed

  1. Open Invoices → Awaiting payment.
  2. Search by invoice #, work order #, or location.
5

Add a sub-vendor

  1. Vendors → Add vendor.
  2. Fill in contact, terms, trades, and notes.
6

Set markup defaults

  1. Settings → set a % per trade.
  2. It becomes the suggestion in the Markup helper.
7

Catch anything stalled

  1. Dashboard → Stuck right now.
  2. Click a stage to jump to those jobs.
