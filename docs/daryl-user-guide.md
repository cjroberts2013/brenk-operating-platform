# Brenk Operating Platform — User Guide

Your dashboard for tracking jobs from ServiceChannel all the way through to
getting paid. Part 1 explains each tab. Part 2 walks through the everyday
tasks step by step.

---

## Part 1 — The tabs at a glance

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **Dashboard** | Morning overview — money tiles, the pipeline, and what's stuck. |
| 📋 **Work Orders** | Every job from ServiceChannel + your own tracking (vendor, notify, pricing). |
| 🧾 **Invoices** | Real SC invoices (Sent → Approved → Paid) + jobs still waiting to be priced. |
| 👷 **Vendors** | Your sub-vendor roster, their jobs, and a schedule calendar. |
| ⚙️ **Settings** | Your default markup % per trade. |

**Getting around:** sign in at **app.brenkfacilityservices.com**; use the left
menu to switch tabs; the top search box filters Work Orders and Vendors (the
Invoices tab has its own search).

---

## Part 2 — How to do the everyday things

### ➊ Handle a new job
1. Open **Work Orders** (new ones show near the top; "Unassigned" is in red).
2. Click the job to open it.
3. Click **Assign a sub-vendor** and pick the right one.
4. Text or call them, then click **Mark notified** so you don't lose track.

### ➋ Price a completed job
1. In **Work Orders** (or the Invoices tab → *Work orders to invoice*), open
   the finished job.
2. In the **Markup helper**, either:
   - enter the vendor's **labor + material cost** and your **markup %**, or
   - flip to **Total bill** and just type the amount you're charging.
3. Check the **Total bill** stays under the **NTE** (it turns red if not).
4. Click **Save**.

### ➌ Invoice it and get paid
1. With the price saved, click **Open in ServiceChannel** and enter the
   invoice there. *(One-click submit is coming; for now you enter it in SC.)*
2. The **Invoices** tab will show it and follow it from **Sent → Approved →
   Paid** on its own.
3. When the client pays, open the job and click **Mark paid** (it also marks
   itself paid automatically once ServiceChannel does).

### ➍ See what's owed to you
1. Open **Invoices**.
2. Stay on the **Awaiting payment** tab to see everything billed but not yet
   paid. Switch to **Paid** for history.
3. Use the **search box** to find one fast by invoice #, work order #, or
   location.

### ➎ Add or update a sub-vendor
1. Open **Vendors** → **Add vendor** (or click an existing one to edit).
2. Fill in contact info, preferred contact method, payment terms, and the
   trades they handle. Add any private notes.
3. Open a vendor any time to see their active jobs and scheduled-work calendar.

### ➏ Set your markup defaults
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Set a default **markup %** for each trade (e.g., doors 85%, plumbing 70%).
3. These show up as a **suggestion** in the Markup helper — you always decide
   the final number.

### ➐ Catch anything stalled
1. On the **Dashboard**, check **Stuck right now** for jobs that have sat too
   long.
2. Click any pipeline stage or money tile to jump straight to those jobs.
